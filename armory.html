01

Shimano Beast Master 6000

Electric Powerhouse

Used for deep-water applications that require electric power and controlled lifting strength. The Beast Master 6000 is suited for targeting deeper species such as Ruby Snapper and large Grouper in waters that may exceed 200 to 300 meters, where sustained retrieval power is important.

02

Shimano Talica 50II

2-Speed Lever Drag

A two-speed offshore reel commonly used for trolling applications. Its gear system allows fast line recovery when needed, then shifts into a lower ratio for increased lifting leverage during heavier fights, making it a strong option for offshore trolling scenarios involving species such as Yellowfin Tuna.

03

Daiwa Saltiga 35N & Saltiga LD35

Monocoque Body Overheads

Prepared for mechanical jigging and high-speed vertical presentations. These reels are selected for smooth retrieve performance, strong structural design, and the line recovery needed for working jigs effectively in offshore conditions, especially when targeting fast and aggressive species such as Giant Trevally.

04

Bullzen Anunaki 50N

Narrow Spool Jigging Reel

A jigging-focused reel built for heavy braid use and strong drag pressure. Its narrow spool format and powerful drag system support controlled pressure against hard-pulling reef species, especially in structure-rich areas where stopping fish early can be important.

05

Cannon Uni-Troll 5 ST

Precision Downrigger System

Used for depth-controlled trolling presentations. With adjustable deployment and retrieval control, this system helps position lures at specific depth bands identified on the sonar, which can be useful when targeting species such as Tanguigue and other offshore pelagics holding below the surface or along current-affected channels.
