01 Morning

Golden Hour Session

Duration 6 Hours
Session Time 5:00 AM – 11:00 AM

A focused daylight session for guests who prefer an early departure and a shorter offshore run. This window is commonly selected for sunrise activity and morning fishing conditions.

Best for Early starters and first-time offshore guests
Environment Cooler temperatures and sunrise visibility
Trip character Focused, efficient, and daylight-based
02 Afternoon

Sunset Drift Session

Duration 6 Hours
Session Time 1:00 PM – 7:00 PM

A half-day session built around late-afternoon movement and a sunset return. This is practical for guests who prefer a later start while still keeping most of the trip within daylight.

Best for Guests who prefer a later departure
Environment Changing afternoon light and sunset conditions
Trip character Relaxed start with a scenic evening return
03 Night Ops

Nocturnal Deep-Sea Session

Duration 12 Hours
Session Time 5:00 PM – 5:00 AM

A night fishing format planned around darker offshore conditions, underwater blue-light support, bait movement, and predator activity after sunset.

Best for Experienced guests interested in night fishing
Environment Dark-water operations and artificial lighting
Trip character Longer, more technical, and more demanding
04 Full-Day

Ultimate Full-Day Expedition

Duration 14 Hours
Session Time 5:00 AM – 7:00 PM

The longest session option, intended for wider route coverage and more time across different feeding windows, fishing areas, and offshore conditions.

Best for Experienced groups wanting maximum trip coverage
Environment Morning, midday, and late-afternoon conditions
Trip character Extended, adaptable, and expedition-focused

How to Choose

Consider more than the clock.

The best session depends on your schedule, preferred fishing style, physical comfort, offshore experience, and desired route coverage.

01

Available time

Decide whether your group prefers a focused half-day trip or a longer offshore commitment.

02

Experience level

Daylight sessions are generally easier for first-time guests, while night and full-day sessions require more stamina.

03

Preferred conditions

Some guests prefer sunrise, others enjoy sunset, and experienced anglers may specifically seek nocturnal conditions.

04

Route flexibility

Longer sessions provide more time to adapt the route, search different grounds, and respond to changing conditions.
