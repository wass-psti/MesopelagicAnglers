Fishing Spots

This page outlines the offshore route areas used for Mesopelagic Anglers charters, including fishing grounds around Mactan, Caubian, Camotes Sea, Middle Pass, and the Danajon Bank route.

Route Selection Logic

Fishing spots are selected based on structure, current movement, session type, target species, and route suitability for the day.

01

Current Breaks

Moving water can create feeding lanes where predators ambush baitfish near reef edges, walls, and pass systems.

02

Drop-Offs & Seamounts

Deeper structure provides access to reef predators, bottom fish, and larger fish holding near underwater terrain changes.

03

Session Matching

Daytime, nocturnal, and full-day sessions are matched with different feeding windows, water movement, and offshore conditions.

The Middle Pass & Caubian Islands

Tier 1 Premium Route

This route focuses on Caubian-side waters, the Middle Pass, reef edges, current breaks, drop-offs, and selected Danajon Bank fishing grounds.

Route Profile

  • Capacity: Maximum 4 anglers
  • Sessions: Morning, Afternoon, Nocturnal Night, or Full-Day Marathon
  • Best For: Private groups, reef-edge routes, current breaks, and Caubian-side fishing areas
01

The Caubian Wall & Deep Drop-Offs

Daytime Targets: Mamsa / Giant Trevally, Tanguigue / Spanish Mackerel, and large Barracuda.

Nighttime Targets: Bigeye Trevally and Barracuda feeding under blue light support.

Techniques: Shallow-water jigging and high-speed trolling.

02

The Outer Barrier Seamounts

Targets: Lapu-Lapu / Grouper, Ahaan / Red Snapper, and Katambak / Longnose Emperor.

Techniques: Slow-pitch vertical jigging.

03

The Middle Pass Drift

Targets: Schooling jacks and migrating Yellowfin Tuna.

Techniques: Light-tackle casting or shallow-water trolling.

The Deep Blue Danajon Banks

Tier 2 Ultra-Premium Route

This route is designed for longer offshore coverage, deeper fishing grounds, and more demanding route planning around the Danajon Bank and Camotes Sea approach.

Route Profile

  • Capacity: Maximum 4 anglers
  • Sessions: Morning, Afternoon, Nocturnal Night, or Full-Day Marathon
  • Best For: Experienced guests, deeper routes, extended coverage, and sportfishing groups
01

The Northeast Pass

Route Area: Camotes Sea border.

Targets: Yellowfin Tuna, Dogtooth Tuna, Tanguigue / Spanish Mackerel, Barracuda, and deep-dwelling Ruby Snapper.

Techniques: High-speed trolling, topwater popping, and deep-drop jigging.

02

The Caubian Middle Pass

Daytime Targets: Mamsa / Giant Trevally, Amberjack, and passing Yellowfin Tuna.

Nighttime Targets: Bigeye Trevally and Barracuda around reef-patrolling areas.

03

The Danajon Deep Drop

Targets: Lapu-Lapu / Grouper, Ahaan / Red Snapper, Dogtooth Tuna, and Amberjack.

Techniques: Slow-pitch vertical jigging and heavy-tackle bottom dropping with electric reel support.
