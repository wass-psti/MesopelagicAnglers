Charter Inclusions & Policies

This page explains what is included in the charter and the policies guests need to understand before booking. These guidelines help protect safety, scheduling, equipment, crew operations, and the private offshore experience.

What the Charter Provides

The charter includes the core operational support needed for a private offshore fishing session. Final setup may vary depending on route, session, tier, sea conditions, and target species.

01

Boat, Fuel & Crew Support

Private vessel operation, captain support, deck assistance, route planning, and offshore execution are included.

02

Fishing Gear & Selected Tackle

Rods, reels, lures, jigs, rigging, and fishing support are prepared based on the selected route and target fish.

03

Cooler & Ice Support

Fish and guest provisions may be stored in the onboard marine cooler with fresh ice during the trip.

04

Basic Media Documentation

Onboard photo or video documentation may be provided when sea conditions and crew workload allow.

Booking & Safety Policies

Guests should review these policies before choosing a session or tier. The goal is to keep expectations clear and make sure the trip remains safe, organized, and manageable for the crew.

01

Capacity Policy

Maximum 4 Guests

The vessel is capped at a maximum of four guests total. This includes anglers, watchers, companions, and photographers. The limit helps maintain comfort, deck space, and safe movement onboard.

02

Weather & Safety Policy

Captain Assessment Required

Trip operation depends on weather, sea state, route suitability, and captain assessment. The captain has final go/no-go authority. Unsafe conditions may require rescheduling or cancellation.

03

Food, Drinks & Guest Conduct

Bring Your Own Provisions

Guests are responsible for bringing their own food and drinks. Alcohol should be kept moderate. Unsafe, impaired, or disruptive behavior may result in the charter being ended for safety reasons.

04

Equipment Responsibility

Normal Loss vs. Negligence

Normal lure loss, line breaks, and fishing wear may occur during offshore trips. However, rods, reels, or major equipment lost or damaged due to negligence may require replacement cost.

Questions Before Booking?

Review the policies before selecting a tier or session. If anything is unclear, check the FAQ or send your booking inquiry with your preferred date, group size, and session.
